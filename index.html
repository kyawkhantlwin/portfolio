"Engineering is not just about building things — it's about building the right things, the right way."

Hi, I'm Kyaw Khant Lwin — a Mechanical Engineering student at Suranaree University of Technology, Thailand, with a burning passion for turning ideas into reality through hands-on engineering.

I specialize in CAD Modelling and Embedded Systems & IoT development — fields where precision meets creativity.
